# Harbourfront Analytics Pte Ltd · Expense policy (made up for this exercise)

1. **Meals.** At most S$50.00 per person per meal: amount divided by attendees. Exactly S$50.00 is allowed.
2. **Taxis.** Reimbursed only if the trip starts between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM, or the purpose names a client.
3. **Receipts.** Any claim above S$100.00 needs a receipt. Exactly S$100.00 does not.
4. **Alcohol.** Never reimbursed, whatever the category.
5. **Deadline.** A claim submitted more than 60 days after the expense date is rejected.
6. **Duplicates.** Same employee, same date, same amount and same merchant: flag the later claim.
